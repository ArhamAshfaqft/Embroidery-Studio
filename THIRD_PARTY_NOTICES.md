# Third-Party Model Notices

## MobileSAM

Raven Embroidery Studio includes MobileSAM-compatible ONNX model files for
local artwork segmentation. Inference runs on the user's device through ONNX
Runtime Web; uploaded artwork is not sent to a hosted inference service.

- Project: <https://github.com/ChaoningZhang/MobileSAM>
- Paper: *Faster Segment Anything: Towards Lightweight SAM for Mobile Applications*
- License: Apache License 2.0
- Copyright: MobileSAM contributors

The bundled browser-compatible encoder and quantized decoder were converted
from MobileSAM and Segment Anything checkpoints. Their inclusion does not imply
endorsement by the original authors.
